# NNNN — Short imperative title

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** who decides this
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Superseded by:** none

## Context

What is true right now, with `file:line` citations. Enough that a reader who was
not in the conversation can judge the decision without opening five other files.

## Decision

One paragraph, past tense once accepted, for example: "Shared business rules live
in X, because Y."

## Options considered

| Option | Cost | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| A | | |
| B | | |

The rejected options matter more than the chosen one. If a future reader cannot
see why the alternative lost, they will try it again.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it costs to reverse.

## How it will be enforced

Which files must agree with this decision, and what command proves it. A decision
that no file enforces is a preference.

## Review

Who approved it, when, and what would cause it to be reopened.
